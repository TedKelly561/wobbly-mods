# 0.39.0 — the jungle on the Big aquarium's top floor (Nov 28)

> Superseded the same day by 0.39.1 to 0.40.0: see `2026-11-28-refinement-pass-0.40.0.md`. The treehouse is lower
> (3 m), trees are sized to fit inside the glass, and the parrots moved. The test list below still applies.

Ted asked for trees on the top floor and what they cost in frame rate. His picks: rainforest giants, about 60,
with birds, a treehouse with a rope bridge, and hanging vines to walk through.

## What is new (top floor only; the other four floors are untouched)
- **60 rainforest giants** (6 shapes, code-built in `Core/src/Shapes/RainforestTree.cs`, picture in
  `docs/previews/forest/sheet.png`). Buttress roots, vines, orange blossoms on half of them. Solid trunks.
  Shorter near the glass walls, where the roof is lower. The old palms and banana trees stay, moved to the edges.
- **Treehouse** in the front right corner: a ramp up (starts near the lagoon, z = 20), a hut on a platform round a
  big tree, a rope bridge to a second tree, and an orange slide back down.
- **11 vine gates** over the paths (walk straight through; nothing solid).
- **25 parrots**: 16 sitting in the trees, 9 flying loops under the roof, plus 3 on the treehouse.
- More made flowers (30).

## Frame rate
- A tree is 4 draw objects and about 2,400 triangles (drawn on both sides). Sixty trees: about 250 objects and
  290,000 triangles on top of a building that already has about 3,900 objects. No new lights.
- New config key `[Aquarium] JungleTrees` (default 60, 2 to 80) for a slower card.
- The log line now also says: "Big aquarium: the rainforest has N giant trees, 11 vine gates and 25 parrots."

## Please test (ROADWS3 session)
1. Place a Big aquarium; go to the top floor. Frame rate before and after looking across the jungle.
2. Trees: standing on the floor (not floating or sunk), none poking through the glass roof, none blocking the ramp,
   the bounce-pad landing, the lagoon walk or the three animal homes.
3. Treehouse: can a Wobbly walk up the ramp, round the trunk, over the bridge and down the slide? Does the slide
   actually slide? Anything to get stuck on?
4. Parrots: right way up, facing the way they fly, wings flapping; sitting ones sit on branches (not in mid-air).
5. Vine gates: placed over the paths, not inside the lagoon rim or the sign.

Not checked in game by the cloud session: all placements above are by arithmetic only.
